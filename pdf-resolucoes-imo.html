II. RCDS should be connected to the ship's position fixing system, to the gyro compass and the speed and distance measuring device. For ships not fitted with a gyro compass, RCDS should be connected to a marine transmitting heading device.

III. RCDS may provide a means to supply SRNC information to external equipment.

A) All are true

B) Only I and II are true

C) Only I and III are true

D) Only II and III are true

E) All are false

Questão 277 HQ - Resoluções IMO: IMO MSC.530(106)

According to IMO Resolution 530 (106), regarding RCDS mode of operation:

I. It should be possible to operate RCDS and all equipment necessary for its normal functioning when supplied by an emergency source of electrical power in accordance with the appropriate requirements SOLAS chapter II-1.

II. Changing from one source of power supply to another should not require the equipment to be manually re-initialized.

III. Any interruption of the power supply for a period of up to 45 seconds should not require the equipment to be manually re-initialized.

A) All are true

B) Only I and II are true

C) Only I and III are true

D) Only II and III are true

E) All are false
